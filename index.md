<section class="block">
  <h2>About</h2>
  <div class="about">
    <p>Before Meshy, I built human motion-capture pipelines at Meta Reality Labs and worked on neural rendering at Adobe Research. I earned my MS in Robotics at Carnegie Mellon, where my thesis explored reconstructing scenes through smoke. These days most of my time goes into scaling 3D models and data and, unglamorously, looking at the data. The single best debugging tool I know is scrolling through a thousand random training samples.</p>
    <p>Off the clock I'm cooking something slightly too ambitious (results vary), watching football (the round-ball kind), staying up way too late for F1 qualifying, or planning the next hike. Lowkey always down to debate any of the above.</p>
  </div>
</section>

        <p>Meta's large-scale multimodal dataset of human motion. I built the multiview capture pipelines, pose and hand-tracking models, and QA filters behind the release. <a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.16258">Paper</a></p>

    <li>
      <span class="work__year">2025</span>
      <div>
        <h3><a href="https://imaging.cs.cmu.edu/smokeseer/">SmokeSeer</a> <span class="tag">3DV 2026</span></h3>
        <p>Seeing through smoke: fuses RGB and thermal video with 3D Gaussian splatting to remove smoke and reconstruct the scene behind it. My MS thesis at CMU. <a href="/smokeseer_supplementary/">Videos</a> · <a href="https://www.ri.cmu.edu/app/uploads/2025/05/Neham_MSR_Thesis_Finally.pdf">Thesis</a></p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="work__year">2024</span>
      <div>
        <h3>Relightable splats for 3D foundation models</h3>
        <p>At Adobe Research I wrote CUDA kernels that replace spherical harmonics with neural features, and scaled training to 32 nodes. Per-asset time went from 6 minutes to 30 seconds.</p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="work__year">2024</span>
      <div>
        <h3><a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3589335.3651484">Counterfactual Explanations of Visual Recommender Systems</a> <span class="tag">WWW 2024</span></h3>
        <p>Explaining visual recommendations through small changes to image embeddings, with language explanations grounded using CLIP. Work from my research internship at Adobe.</p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="work__year">2022</span>
      <div>
        <h3><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14821">Towards Realistic Underwater Dataset Generation and Color Restoration</a> <span class="tag">ICVGIP 2022</span></h3>
        <p>Bridging synthetic and real underwater imagery with image-to-image translation, creating a more realistic training dataset for underwater color restoration. Research at IIT Madras.</p>
