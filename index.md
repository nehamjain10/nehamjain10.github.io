## About

I'm a Researcher at [Meshy.ai](https://www.meshy.ai/) working on generative models for 3D — multimodal conditioning, training, and the evaluation and data systems they depend on. Before Meshy I was a Research Engineer at [Meta Reality Labs](https://about.meta.com/realitylabs/), where I shipped the data pipelines behind [Embody3D](https://www.meta.com/emerging-tech/codec-avatars/embody-3d/), and I interned twice at Adobe Research, writing CUDA kernels to make relightable Gaussian-splat training 12× faster.

I hold an MS in Robotics from [Carnegie Mellon](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/), advised by [Prof. Ioannis Gkioulekas](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~igkioule/) in collaboration with [Prof. Sebastian Scherer](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/ri-faculty/sebastian-scherer/), and a BTech in Electrical Engineering from [IIT Madras](https://www.iitm.ac.in/). I'm always open to research discussions and collaborations — reach me at **nehamjain2002 [at] gmail.com**.
